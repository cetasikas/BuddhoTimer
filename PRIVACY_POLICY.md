# Privacy Policy for Buddho Timer

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

Buddho Timer ("the App") is developed as a distraction-free, 100% offline meditation and breathwork timer. We respect your privacy and believe your spiritual practice is personal.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Buddho Timer **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or identifiable information**.

- **No Analytics or Trackers**: We do not use third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics).
- **No Advertisements**: There are no ads or advertising SDKs.
- **No User Accounts**: You do not need to sign in or create an account.
- **No Internet Access**: The App does not request internet permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`) and operates entirely on your local device.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage
All meditation session logs, notes, timer configurations, and personal streaks are stored strictly on your local device using an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database. 
- You have full ownership of your data.
- If you export a backup (JSON/CSV), the file is saved locally to your device storage where you choose.
- If you uninstall the app or clear app data, all local data is deleted.

---

### 3. Device Permissions Used
The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary for timer functionality:
- **Notifications & Foreground Service**: To display your ongoing meditation countdown and overtime timer in your status bar.
- **Vibration**: To play gentle haptic cues when starting or completing a session.
- **Do Not Disturb (DND) Access (Optional)**: To automatically silence incoming phone calls and notifications during meditation, if enabled by you in Settings.
- **Battery Optimization Exemption (Optional)**: To ensure Android does not kill your meditation timer during long sitting sessions.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because Buddho Timer does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.
