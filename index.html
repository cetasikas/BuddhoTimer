Features

Everything needed for a steady sit.

Built for reliability during practice and restraint outside of it.

Reliable timer

Foreground service, wake lock, notifications, open-ended sessions, preparation countdown, and continuous overrun mode.

Sound and guidance

Ambient soundscapes, acoustic chimes, guided meditations, and breathing practice support.

Local journal

Markdown reflections, mood notes, search, filters, JSON export, Markdown export, heatmap, and insights.

Gentle intentions

Daily practice intention and rest-day handling without guilt-driven streak mechanics.

Teachings

Simple practice instructions and short teaching quotes for reflection before or after meditation.

Home widget

A calm Android widget showing today's practice rhythm and a simple prompt to return when ready.

Privacy

Local by default.

Buddho Timer does not need an account or server. Your timer history and journal stay on your device unless you export or back them up yourself.

  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No telemetry
  • No user accounts
  • No social login
  • No required cloud
